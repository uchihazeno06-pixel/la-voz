Welcome.

This is where I write. World events. Personal thoughts. Things I am turning over. No algorithm decides what you see here. Just whatever I have finished and hit publish on.

A few things about what this is:

- A voice. La Voz means The Voice in Spanish. That is the whole idea.
- A pen name. I am Zeno. That is not my real name. I am a university student, and I would like to keep my face and my legal identity off the internet for now.
- Text-first. No videos of myself. No social media circus. Just writing.

If you are here, thank you. Read what is useful, skip what is not.
