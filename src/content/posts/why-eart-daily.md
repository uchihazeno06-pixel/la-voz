I made a page called **Earth Daily** because I got tired of only hearing about the world through whatever the feed decided I should care about that day.

It is not a news site. It is not analysis. It is a dispatch. One person, watching what is happening, writing down what seems worth writing down.

Sometimes several a day. Sometimes none. No schedule, because the world does not have one.
